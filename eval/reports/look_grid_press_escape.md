# Take his eyes: the look grid on `press_escape`

Generated by `uv run python -m eval.look_grid --scenario press_escape` (SPEC 3.7, 7). The viewer gives the hero one look, from the pass to him until his decision; his own looks in that window do not happen. Directions are degrees from the ball (150 is the widest a scan turns); times are back from the reception, every 0.4 s. Every look is a re-simulation with keyed randomness, so a look changes the run only through what he sees.
