A teaching scenario (SPEC 3.6), in the coach's order. **The look times are scripted for teaching; what he sees, believes and does follows the sim.** He starts facing his own half, toward the ball (his forward view, not a scan). His looks (direction from where his body faces, seconds before the planned reception): left 120° at 3.2 s, right -120° at 1.5 s. Rule 1, 0.8 s after the left look's scripted start: if he believes an opponent will be within 7 m of him when the ball arrives, he drops back toward the ball, up to 3 m, to receive with space. Rule 2, 0.8 s after the right look's: if he believes one will be within 6 m, he receives on the half-turn, 70° side-on, away from him. All four runs share every random draw (SPEC 3.7). Generated by `uv run python -m eval.pro_scan`.

Limits: the look times are scripted for teaching; what he sees, believes and does follows the sim. The two rules are scoped to this scenario and act on his belief only. The sim has no first touch or reception mechanics, so the half-turn changes his body angle and the options open to him next (where the ball sits, where his eyes rest while he has it, what an opponent can win ball-first), not the touch.
