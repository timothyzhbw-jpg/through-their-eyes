His looks (direction from where his body faces, seconds before the planned reception): left 120° at 3.2 s, right -120° at 1.5 s. Rule 1, 0.8 s after the left look's scripted start: if he believes an opponent will be within 7 m of him when the ball arrives, he drops back toward the ball, up to 3 m, to receive with space. Rule 2, 0.8 s after the right look's: if he believes one will be within 6 m, he receives on the half-turn, 70° side-on, away from him. All four runs share every random draw (SPEC 3.7). Generated by `uv run python -m eval.pro_scan`.
